Join the Work

Get Involved

We're in our founding season — which means the earliest people who show up help shape what MySGA becomes. Whether you have an hour, a skill, a connection, or a voice, there's a place for you here.

Volunteer With Us

We're shaping our volunteer structure right now, and we want founding volunteers involved from the start. We need help with event-day support, sorting and organizing donated goods, outreach and nomination review, and administrative planning.

Email Us to Volunteer

Partner With Us

Churches, schools, businesses, and community organizations that share our mission — we want to hear from you. Partnership can mean hosting an initiative, referrals, in-kind support, or co-promotion.

Start a Partnership Conversation

Sponsor an Initiative

We're not yet accepting monetary donations — that opens once our federal tax-exempt status is confirmed. But several Q4 2026 initiatives run on in-kind and event-specific sponsorship right now: supplies, meals, venues, and professional services.

Our Q4 2026 Community Initiatives

These six initiatives were approved by our Board on August 5, 2026 — our first full season of community impact work. Some details, including exact dates and a few team leads, are still being finalized. These are our founding-season goals, not results already achieved — we'll update this page with real outcomes as each initiative runs.

Domestic Violence Awareness Walk

Organized by Dr. Sharon Stafford in memory of LaSmockie Fountain. This will be MySGA's third year participating. We're recruiting walkers now.
