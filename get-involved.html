Join the Work

Get Involved

We're in our founding season — which means the earliest people who show up help build the foundation of our work. Whether you have an hour, a skill, a connection, or a voice, there's a place for you here.

Volunteer With Us

We're building our volunteer program from the ground up, and we want founding volunteers involved from the start. Right now we need help with event support and organizing donated goods. Tell us a little about your time and skills, and we'll follow up as opportunities come available.

Email Us to Volunteer

Partner With Us

Churches, schools, businesses, and community organizations that share our mission — we want to hear from you. Partnership can mean hosting an initiative, referrals, in-kind support, or co-promotion.

Start a Partnership Conversation

Sponsor an Initiative

Several of our community initiatives welcome in-kind and event-specific sponsorship: supplies, meals, shirts, venues, and professional services.

Email Us About Sponsoring

Current Opportunities

We're currently building out a handful of community initiatives — including a local awareness walk, a meal for families facing medical hardship, health-education gatherings, a classroom-support project, and a prom dress drive. If you'd like to support a specific effort, email us and we'll share current ways to help.
