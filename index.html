Before the Nonprofit

A Legacy of Showing Up

MySGA is a new nonprofit corporation — but the mission behind it isn't new. For years, this work has shown up one relationship at a time, through humanitarian outreach, crisis response, mentorship, and quiet, consistent support for women, families, and communities in need. MySGA is the structure now being built to sustain and expand that same spirit of service.

Our Approach

Three commitments, one alliance

01

Educate

We provide access to education and knowledge-building that opens doors — from leadership training to practical life skills.

02

Equip

We equip women and girls with mentorship, workforce readiness, and tools to build sustainable futures for themselves and their families.

03

Empower

We empower our sisters to lead — in their communities, their careers, and their advocacy — locally, nationally, and internationally.
